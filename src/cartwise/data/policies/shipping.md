# Shipping

## Standard shipping
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. It is free on orders of $50 or more; orders under $50 pay a flat $5.99.

## Express shipping
Express shipping arrives in 1 to 2 business days for a flat $12.99 on any order size. Orders placed before 2 p.m. Eastern ship the same day.

## Where we ship
We ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship to PO boxes for laptops, or internationally.
