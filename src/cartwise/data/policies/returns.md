# Returns and refunds

## Standard returns
Most unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the return is received.

## Opened electronics
Opened headphones and laptops can be returned within 15 days of delivery. Opened laptops carry a 15% restocking fee unless the item is defective.

## Final sale
Items marked final sale, gift cards and opened coffee consumables cannot be returned.

## Exchanges
Exchanges for a different size or color are free within 30 days. Running shoes can be exchanged once if they show no outdoor wear.
